Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle

About The Movie


Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle begins at the moment the war with the demons turns decisive. After Muzan Kibutsuji attacks the Demon Slayer Corps’ headquarters, Tanjiro Kamado and the Hashira are dragged into the Infinity Castle, a shifting, impossible fortress that bends space around its master. Rooms rearrange and floors become walls. Every Demon Slayer is separated, and every one of them lands in front of an enemy.

The castle belongs to the Upper Moons, the strongest demons under Muzan, and the survivors must face them one by one. Shinobu Kocho meets Doma, the demon who murdered her sister. She has spent years preparing to avenge her, and she is willing to give up her own life to do it. Elsewhere, Zenitsu Agatsuma faces Kaigaku, a former fellow student who chose power over loyalty and became everything his old master despised. Zenitsu has to settle old questions about courage and the teacher who believed in him.

At the center of the arc, Tanjiro and Giyu Tomioka fight Akaza, the Upper Moon who killed Kyojuro Rengoku. The fight is brutal. Akaza is faster and stronger than both of them, and he heals from wounds that would kill any human. Tanjiro pushes past his limits and begins to sense the world around him with a new clarity. As the battle goes on, the story steps into Akaza’s past and shows the human life he lost, the people he loved, and the grief that twisted him into what he became.
